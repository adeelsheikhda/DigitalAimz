## Section 4: Anchor Text Optimization Framework (2025–2026 Standards)

### What Scores 9–10/10
**SEO:** `[Service] + [Geo] + [Qualifier]` — "reliable Frankfurt airport transfer service"  
**AEO:** Complete question-answering phrases — "how to book an airport transfer in Frankfurt"  
**GEO:** Named entity + location + category — "Halcyon Aesthetics anti-wrinkle clinic Dubai"  
**RAG/LLMs:** Self-contained semantic phrases with no pronoun dependency — "anonymous Instagram story viewer that requires no login"

### What Scores 0–2/10 (Avoid)
- Generic CTAs: "Click here", "Visit Site", "Get Code", "Homepage", "Read more"
- Naked URLs without keyword context
- Spam-pattern anchors (branded URL from 100+ RDs, link-selling scheme anchors)
- Navigation artifacts: "Book Appointment Logo 1 Logo 2 Logo 3..."
- Anchors longer than one sentence
