80/20 Priority Matrix
Do Q1 first. Delegate Q2. Schedule Q3. Eliminate Q4.
The Rule as Team Lead:
20% of your SEO tasks drive 80% of ranking results.
20% of your pages drive 80% of revenue.
20% of your team effort drives 80% of client value.

Your job = find that 20% every Monday and build the whole week around it.
Weekly Rhythm:
Mon: Pull GSC drops → assign fixes
Tue–Thu: Team executes. You unblock.
Fri: Review wins + set next week's focus

Block 90 min Mon morning. This is your highest leverage hour.
SEO Work Priority Matrix
Q1 — DO FIRST (High Impact, Easy Win)
Fix title/meta on money pages dropping
Resolve GSC crawl errors on key URLs
Add internal links to ranking pages
Pos 5–15 pages with 1k+ impressions
Update outdated content (year, stats)
Q2 — DELEGATE (High Impact, Hard Work)
Full technical audit + fix implementation
New long-form content creation
Link building outreach campaigns
Core Web Vitals optimisation (dev task)
Schema markup implementation
Q3 — SCHEDULE (Low Impact, Easy)
Weekly client reports
Social signals / minor off-page tasks
Image alt text bulk updates
Sitemap resubmission (routine)
Minor blog meta optimisation
Q4 — ELIMINATE (Low Impact, Hard)
Chasing DA 5 backlinks
Optimising pages with 0 impressions
Redesigning pages with good rankings
Keyword research for zero-vol terms
Manual disavow for low-risk links
Daily Brief
Your 15-min morning standup checklist. Run this every day before 10am.
08:30 — MONDAY (5 min)
1. Open GSC → Filter last 7 days vs prev 7 days. Identify any page that dropped 2+ positions.
2. Flag money pages with position change. These go to top of task board immediately.
3. Check crawl coverage — any new errors since last week?
DAILY — Every Morning (5 min)
Check your team: Did yesterday's tasks get done?
Unblock anyone waiting on you for approvals or content.
Re-prioritise if a project had a sudden ranking change overnight.
One Slack message to the team with today's top 3 focus items.
FRIDAY — Weekly Wrap (10 min)
Record wins: Which tasks moved rankings?
Record failures: What took time with no result? Cut it.
Set next week's Q1 — your 3 highest-impact tasks.
Send client report for critical projects.
TEAM LEAD RULE — Always
You don't do execution. You direct it.
Your job is: assign → unblock → review → decide.
If you're spending hours on 1 task yourself, you're operating as an individual contributor, not a lead.
Protect your time for strategy and decisions only.
