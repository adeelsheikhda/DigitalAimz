Yes — shaving between sessions is completely fine and actually recommended. You should avoid waxing, plucking, or threading as these remove the hair root that the laser needs to target. Shaving leaves the follicle intact.

Shave the treatment area 24 hours before your appointment. Avoid sun exposure, tanning beds, and self-tanner for at least 2 weeks prior. Come with clean, product-free skin. Avoid caffeine immediately before if you are sensitive to warmth sensations.

Laser hair removal produces long-lasting to permanent reduction — not always 100% permanent for every individual hair. Most clients achieve 85–95% reduction that is permanent after completing their course. Occasional maintenance sessions (once a year) may be recommended for hormonal areas.

For 24–48 hours post-treatment: avoid sun exposure, hot showers, saunas, and intense exercise. Apply the provided soothing gel to any redness. SPF 50 on exposed areas is essential. Hair will continue to shed over 1–3 weeks post-session — this is normal and expected.
